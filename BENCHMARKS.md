# Benchmark evidence and limitations - MorphTree 1.0.0

No independent MorphTree **1.0.0** competitive benchmark, predictive advantage or generalized speed/RAM benefit is claimed. Previous authorized LIVE trials validated the bounded evolutionary **execution** for binary, multiclass and regression on small synthetic datasets, including meaningful rejected mutations, freeze, numerical trials and inference/persistence. They were **not** an independent predictive-benefit study.

See the [research findings](research/FINDINGS.md), [experiment registry](research/EXPERIMENTS.md) and [evidence levels](research/VALIDATION_LEVELS.md). The owner retains full historic benchmark reports, inputs, negative outcomes and provenance in the separate unabridged archive. Hardware, protocols and budgets differ across historic experiments; do not pool their results as a first-release benchmark.

For a future meaningful comparison, pre-register external datasets, splits, task metrics, costs and baseline presets; use independent held-out evaluation; include initial family trials, OOF costs, all structural candidates, Optuna and final training in the **total** MorphTree budget. Competitor engines must remain external benchmarks only.
