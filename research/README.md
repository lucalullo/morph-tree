# MorphTree research

This **curated public research portal** summarizes the scientific and engineering record without mirroring thousands of raw development files. The full unabridged archival files are retained separately in the owner-controlled MorphTree development workspace. Nothing has been reclassified as a positive result for publication.

## Navigate

- [Research chronology](HISTORY.md): early R1-R20 and subsequent MORPH/COMP/PRODUCT milestones, including negative outcomes.
- [Experiment index](EXPERIMENTS.md): the **210** registered original experiment IDs and their recorded verdicts; links to omitted original reports are textual archive references, not public downloads.
- [Evidence metadata](EVIDENCE_INDEX.json): retained registry, original IDs, units and private evidence SHA-256 handles. Original `report` and `protocol` paths refer to the **full** archive and need not exist in this curated repository.
- [Evidence interpretation](VALIDATION_LEVELS.md): distinguish mathematical tests, software contracts, saved-weight replay, LIVE fits and independent confirmation.
- [Negative results and open research](FINDINGS.md): selected scientifically relevant failures and constraints.
- [Private evidence boundary](PRIVATE_EVIDENCE.md): restricted materials excluded from GitHub.
- [Canonical MorphTree vision](../docs/Relazione_MorphTree.txt) and [release qualification](../docs/QUALIFICATION.md).

The source archive is the authoritative location for detailed experiment protocols, metrics and retained receipts. Summary status must not be interpreted as a new validation or claim of statistical independence. More than one record can concern the same dataset or scientific question.

**Research remains inconclusive on independent predictive benefit from structural evolution.** A correctly rejected descendant is an expected outcome, not evidence of general accuracy leadership.
