# Research findings and remaining questions

This is a **curated overview**, not a new experiment or a replacement for the original reports. The [full registry](EVIDENCE_INDEX.json), [chronology](HISTORY.md) and separate owner archive retain original verdicts, including failures and censored outcomes.

## Results that constrain claims

- **R1-R20:** tested diagnostic guidance, bandit controls, composition, capacity heuristics, structural selection fidelity and species-boundary hypotheses did **not** establish a consistently better and sufficiently affordable generalization method under their recorded gates. Distinct rounds reuse some panels; they are not independent replications.
- **MC-001:** the studied softmax preference did not pass the multiclass quality/tail gate. This does not reject multinomial boosting generally.
- **LL-001:** the studied terminal-region correction failed the complete quality gate; isolated positive signals did not justify a default.
- **AUTO-001/AUTO-002:** studied adaptive/capacity variants failed promotion gates against strong or tuned controls. Positive improvements against weaker references do not imply competitiveness.
- **PERF studies:** some local engineering improvements were retained, while JIT, compiled-kernel, memory and cost-related proposals often failed combined speed/RAM/cold-start gates. Hardware- and protocol-specific effects must not be generalized.
- **INT018/INT019:** real three-task paths ran; observed structural descendants were **rejected appropriately**. This supports execution and rollback, not independent proof of adaptive improvement.

## Open research

Important remaining work includes additional native learner families and structurally different mutation operators, diagnosis-to-mutation causal value, measured efficiency of full end-to-end fit, prospective generalization on new datasets, safer cost estimates and broader platform qualification. The first public 1.0.0 scope does not require benchmark superiority.

The [canonical definition](../docs/Relazione_MorphTree.txt) is the project goal. It is not a claim that all catalogued methods are already implemented or validated.
