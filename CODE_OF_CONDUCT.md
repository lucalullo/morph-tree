# Code of conduct

MorphTree welcomes focused, constructive technical collaboration. Discussions
should be respectful, evidence-based and considerate of different experience
levels. Harassment, discriminatory attacks, threats, doxxing, spam and exposing
private datasets or personal details are not acceptable.

Maintain project and third-party licenses, acknowledge uncertainty in claims,
and report security vulnerabilities through the private channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not as public issues.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, close contributions or restrict access to
protect participants. Share sensitive conduct concerns through a private
maintainer channel when one is available, rather than posting personal details.
